01 — The big questions

Thoughts in progress.

There are no perfect answers—just ideas worth carrying with us.

01

How will you begin to dream something no machine can dream?

I will notice small things in daily life, like a conversation or a new feeling. I will stay curious and try ideas, even if they do not work.

✳
02

If memory is built from shared stories, which dreams do you want written into the past?

I want to remember trying new things and sharing time with people I love. I hope my story shows courage, curiosity, and growth.

✳
03

What remains of you when all your stories are rewritten?

My values and the way I treat other people still matter. I want to stay kind, curious, and open to different ideas.

✳
04

How will you ensure AI remains faithful to your will?

I will use AI as a tool, not let it make choices for me. I will check its ideas and decide what fits my own goals.

✳

02 — A life, in details

The everyday magic.

A meaningful future is also made of ordinary days, familiar places, and people we care about.

♡

Which relationships will make your life meaningful and magical? How do you nurture them?

Close friends and family make life feel meaningful to me. I will care for these relationships by listening, spending time together, and showing that I appreciate them.

⌂

What are the aesthetics of the places where you live your life, and how did they come to be that way?

I like places that feel warm, bright, and a little personal. Their look comes from the people, memories, and objects that fill them.

✎

How do you spend your time? What stories do you seek out—to tell or to listen to?

I want to make time for learning, creating, and being with people. I enjoy honest stories that help me understand lives different from mine.

♫

How will it sound? What will the rhythm of your day be?

I hope my days have a calm start, focused work, and time to rest. Their sound might be music, quiet moments, and friendly voices.

↗

How will you move through the world?

I want to move through the world with care and an open mind. I will try to notice what others need and make space for different points of view.

◎

What stories do you seek out, whether to share or to absorb and pass on?

I seek stories about real people, change, and hope. I want to share the ones that make someone feel seen or inspire a useful conversation.
